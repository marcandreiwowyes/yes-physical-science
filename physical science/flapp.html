FLAPPY BIRD

LESSON

Flappy Bird shows that Newton's laws of motion can be observed in simple games and everyday activities.
The bird's movement demonstrates inertia, acceleration, and action-and-reaction forces. By understanding
laws, we can see that changes in motion are caused by forces acting on an object. The game teaches us that
science is not only found in textbooks, it can also be seen in technology, games, and the world around us.

INERTIA

When the bird is not flapping, it continues moving according to its current motion. It does not suddenly change direction unless a force acts on it.

ACCELERATION

When the player taps the screen, the bird receives an upward force and accelerates upward.

ACTION & REACTION

When the bird flaps its wings downwrd it pushes air dowmward. The air produces an upward reaction force that helps the bird rise
